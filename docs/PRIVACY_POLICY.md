# Hogona — Privacy policy

_Updated 24 October 2026. The same text is shown in the app under Settings → Read the full privacy policy (`src/constants/legal.ts`). Host this page at a public URL and paste that URL into App Store Connect and Google Play Console._

**The short version.** Hogona has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no server of its own. We don't collect, sell or share your personal data, and we don't track you across apps or websites.

**What stays on your phone.** Your preferences, language, home and work places, saved places, recent searches and the list of apps you opened from Hogona are stored only on this device. You can erase all of it any time in Settings → "Erase everything on this phone", or by uninstalling the app.

**Location.** Hogona asks for location only when you tap "Use my location" or turn on a stop alert. "Use my location" sets your start point and finds nearby stations; to name the spot, the coordinates are sent once to the address-search service below. A stop alert watches your position on the phone while the journey screen is open, to vibrate before your stop; it stops when you leave the screen and nothing is sent anywhere. Location is never stored, tracked in the background or shared with us.

**Online services Hogona talks to.**
- Address search — Photon by komoot (OpenStreetMap data): receives what you type and, if you use your location, the coordinates.
- Street paths — OSRM routing by FOSSGIS: receives the start and end of a walking or driving leg.
- Web map tiles — OpenStreetMap.
- Rain notice — Open-Meteo: receives the rounded coordinates (about 100 m) of your trip's start point to return a 3-hour forecast.

These requests are made only to answer your request and carry no account or identifier from us. Each service has its own privacy policy.

**Other apps.** When you tap Uber, Rapido, Namma Metro, Namma BMTC, WhatsApp or Google Maps, Hogona opens that app with your trip details (for example pickup and drop). Anything you do there — booking, payment, chat — is governed by that company's own terms and privacy policy. Hogona never books, pays or reads anything inside those apps.

**Children.** Hogona is a general travel tool and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

**Changes.** If this policy changes, the new version will be shown in the app with a new date.

**Contact.** Set `EXPO_PUBLIC_SUPPORT_EMAIL` and add the same address here before publishing.
